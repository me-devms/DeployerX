# Technology Logo Sources

- `electron.svg`, `nodejs.svg`, `sqlite.svg`, and `firebase.svg` are sourced from the [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) project.
- `xterm.png` is the xterm.js project mark from the [xterm.js repository](https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/master/demo/logo.png).
- `novnc.svg` is the official noVNC icon from the [noVNC repository](https://github.com/novnc/noVNC/blob/master/app/images/icons/novnc-icon.svg).
- `ssh2.svg` and `monaco.svg` are DeployerX-created product symbols because those projects do not publish standalone logo marks suitable for this card treatment.

The technology names and logos remain the property of their respective owners and are used only to identify the technologies used by DeployerX.
